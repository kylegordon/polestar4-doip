1BB3:7022 (left lamp) and 1BB4:7022 (right lamp): 32 brightness bytes each, drawn as 2 rows of 16 segments, left to right as seen from the driver's seat. Upper row (bytes 0-15) = main beam: lit only above about 29 mph in auto, brightest in the middle. Lower row (bytes 16-31) = the matrix base pattern, lit whenever the car moves in the dark; its right-hand (offside) end is the part cut for oncoming traffic. Layout inferred from 390 recorded frames (2026-11-14), not yet confirmed by a test. Dark segments are shaded out (an oncoming car, a vehicle ahead or a reflective sign); the dipped beam used when stationary is not in this data.
